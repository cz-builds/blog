Hi, I'm CZ. I'm an IC (Integrated Circuit) student working with FPGA and embedded systems.

I'm interested in the intersection of **AI and hardware** — how to make neural networks run on resource-constrained chips, and what that means for the future of computing.

This blog is where I document what I learn, build, and think about. Topics include:

- AI inference on FPGA (Zynq-7020)
- Hardware architecture for neural networks
- The AI industry from a hardware perspective
- Lessons from building software projects

I build in public. Follow along on [GitHub](https://github.com/xcz1404-glitch) and [Twitter/X](https://twitter.com/cz_build).
